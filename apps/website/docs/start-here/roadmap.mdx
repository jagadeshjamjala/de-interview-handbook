# Roadmap

The handbook is built in the open. Sections appear only when they're written, with no empty pages. This page lists what's coming.

## Next release

- **DE 75 study planner.** A week-by-week plan from 75 curated questions, filtered by your level, role type, focus areas and hours per week, with progress tracking, a shareable link and a calendar export. 55 of the 75 questions are live today.
- **Mock interview mode.** Timed sessions with spoilers locked, rubric self-scoring and a debrief that feeds your weak topics back into the planner, plus a "copy as AI mock prompt" button.
- **Level switcher.** Pick your level once and every page highlights what matters for it.

## Planned sections

- **Tool deep dives:** Spark, Kafka, Airflow, dbt, Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, Iceberg and Delta.
- **Data quality & observability:** tests, contracts, anomaly detection, SLAs.
- **Incident drills:** "the dashboard is wrong", "the job is 3 hours late", as branching scenarios.
- **Lead / Staff / EM track:** platform strategy, build vs buy, cost (FinOps), team design and hiring, leadership behavioral.
- **Offer:** DE levels across companies, compensation and negotiation. This section waits until it can be backed by real, cited sources.
- **More questions:** 5 Python, 5 pipeline system design (real-time fraud features, GDPR delete across a lake, multi-tenant SaaS analytics, cutting a runaway warehouse bill, legacy to cloud migration) and 10 tool questions.

## Later

A runnable Python playground, a pipeline design canvas, a skill self-assessment, and spaced-repetition flashcards from question follow-ups.

## Contribute

Found a wrong answer, or have a question from a real interview? The handbook is open source and contributions are welcome. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/jagadeshjamjala/de-interview-handbook/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) on GitHub. Every SQL answer is checked automatically against a reviewed expected result before it's merged.
