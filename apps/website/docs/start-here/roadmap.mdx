# Roadmap

The handbook is built in the open. Sections appear only when they're written, with no empty pages. This page lists what's coming.

## Live now

- **[DE 75 study planner](/planner)**: a week-by-week plan from 75 curated questions for your level, role type and time, with progress tracking, a shareable link and a calendar export. All 75 questions are live.
- **[Mock interview mode](/mock)**: timed sessions, rubric self-scoring, and a debrief that feeds weak topics into the planner, plus a "copy as AI mock prompt" button.
- **[Tool deep dives](../rounds/tools/index.mdx)**: Spark, Kafka, Airflow, dbt, Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks and table formats, each with practice questions.
- **[Data warehouse engineering](../rounds/warehouse-engineering.mdx)**: columnar storage, MPP distribution, query plans, loading and workload management.
- **[Data quality & observability](../rounds/data-quality.mdx)**: tests, data contracts, anomaly detection, freshness SLAs and stopping bad data before it's published.
- **[Lead / Staff / EM track](../lead-track/index.mdx)**: platform strategy, build vs buy, cost (FinOps), team design and hiring, and leadership behavioral stories.
- **Python playground**: run the Python questions in your browser against their tests.
- **[Flashcards](/flashcards)**: spaced repetition over the questions' 176 follow-ups, each with a short model answer.
- **[Incident drills](/drills)**: branching "what do you check next?" scenarios (a late DAG, a wrong metric, Kafka lag).
- **[Pipeline design canvas](/canvas)**: sketch source → serve, then self-review against the questions interviewers ask.
- **[Skill radar](/radar)**: rate yourself per skill; weak skills seed the planner.
- **Level switcher**: pick your level in the navbar. The sidebar dims pages not tagged for it, and every page highlights your row in the level box.

## Contribute

Found a wrong answer, or have a question from a real interview? The handbook is open source and contributions are welcome. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/jagadeshjamjala/de-interview-handbook/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) on GitHub. Every SQL answer is checked automatically against a reviewed expected result before it's merged.
