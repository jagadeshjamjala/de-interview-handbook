# The DE 75 planner

**[Open the planner →](/planner)**

DE 75 is a curated list of 75 questions across SQL, Python, data modeling, pipeline system design, tools and behavioral. It's ordered so that fundamentals come before the patterns built on them. All 75 are live.

## How your plan is built

1. **Filter.** Questions tagged for your level, in the rounds you checked under *Focus areas*.
2. **Fit to your time.** Each question gets twice its interview time: one attempt plus reviewing the solution and rubric. If they don't all fit in *weeks × hours*, the planner keeps the most useful ones for you: weak topics from your mock interviews first, then questions marked for your role type, then (for senior and lead) hard, design and behavioral questions.
3. **Schedule.** The kept questions stay in curated order and fill each week's hours before the next week starts.

**Role packs.** Each role type also adds a small pack of extra questions for that role (for example warehouse tuning for a data warehouse engineer). They come after the 75 in study order and count as role questions when time is short.

The settings live in the page URL, so **the link reproduces the exact same plan** for anyone who opens it. Your check-marks stay in your browser only.

## The calendar file

*Download calendar (.ics)* creates one event per study day, at your start time, with your weekly hours split evenly across the days. Each event lists that week's questions with links. It works with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook.

## Mock interviews feed the plan

1. Run a [mock interview](/mock): pick a round, level and length (30, 45 or 60 minutes). Questions come one at a time against the clock.
2. At the end, compare with the model answers and mark each rubric point *strong*, *partial* or *missed*. SQL questions count 60% for a correct auto-checked result and 40% for the rubric.
3. The debrief lists your **weak topics**: the topic of every missed point, plus all topics of any question under 60%.
4. **Add weak topics to my planner** reopens your plan with those topics prioritized.

## Flashcards for follow-ups

Interviewers rarely stop at the first answer. **[Flashcards](/flashcards)** turn every question's follow-ups into a spaced-repetition deck: answer out loud, reveal, rate yourself, and the cards you find hard come back sooner. Ten new cards a day by default.

## Mock mode everywhere

The **Mock mode** toggle in the navbar locks every solution and hides hints across the whole site, and adds a timer to each question. Use it when you practice from the question bank or the handbook pages.

Prefer practicing with an AI chatbot? On the mock page, **Copy as AI mock prompt** gives you an interviewer prompt that asks the questions one at a time and grades against the same rubric.
