# Lead, Staff and EM interviews

At lead level the question changes from "can you build this?" to **"can you decide what should be built, get many people to build it, and show it mattered?"** You'll still get technical rounds, but they're judged on judgment, scope and influence more than on syntax.

## Two tracks, different loops

| | Staff / principal (IC) | Engineering manager |
|---|---|---|
| **Main question** | Can you set technical direction across teams? | Can you build a team that delivers and grows? |
| **Technical rounds** | Deep: large system design, architecture reviews, sometimes coding | Lighter: design discussion, judging trade-offs, reviewing others' designs |
| **Behavioral focus** | Influence without authority, technical strategy, mentoring, hard trade-offs | Hiring, performance, team structure, delivery, stakeholder management |
| **Evidence they want** | Designs and standards other teams adopted | Teams you built, people you grew, outcomes your team delivered |

Many companies blend the two for "data lead" roles. Ask the recruiter which competencies each round covers, and prepare for both if it's unclear.

## How you're evaluated

- **Scope:** the size of the problems you owned (one pipeline → a platform → an organization's data strategy).
- **Ambiguity:** whether you turned a vague goal into a plan with priorities and measures.
- **Influence:** whether other teams changed what they did because of you.
- **Judgment:** trade-offs you made explicitly, including what you chose *not* to do.
- **Impact:** numbers: cost saved, incidents reduced, delivery time, revenue enabled, people promoted.

## How to prepare

1. **Build 6–8 lead-level stories** (see [Leadership behavioral](./leadership-behavioral.mdx)), each with a business problem, your decision, how you brought others along, and a measured result.
2. **Prepare a strategy narrative** for your current or last platform: where it was, what you changed and why, what's next. See [Platform strategy](./platform-strategy.mdx).
3. **Practice large designs** with an emphasis on trade-offs, migration path and cost: the [pipeline system design](../rounds/system-design/index.mdx) questions marked lead.
4. **Managers:** prepare your hiring process, a team structure you designed, and a difficult performance situation. See [Team design & hiring](./team-design-and-hiring.mdx).
